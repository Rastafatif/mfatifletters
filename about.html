Hello. I'm Fatifff, and this is where I keep my letters — short essays written as if to a friend, about the things I notice and the things I make.

Most letters start with one small observation: a sound in a room, a pattern in a set of numbers, a building that feels different from the one next door. I try to look at it slowly, draw it, and write down what I learn.

This site is kept black and white on purpose. Fewer colours means more attention on the words and the drawings.
